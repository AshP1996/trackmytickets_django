Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our pricing model.

Is TrackMyTickets really free?
Yes, 100% free. There are no hidden charges, no trial periods, and no feature gates. You get full access to every feature from day one — multi-tenant architecture, analytics, knowledge base, and more.
Do I need a credit card to sign up?
No. Registration requires only your organization name, email, and a password. You can start managing tickets within minutes.
Are there any usage limits?
There are no limits on the number of tickets, projects, or knowledge base articles. User limits are configurable per organization by your admin.
Will there be paid plans in the future?
We may introduce premium enterprise features like SSO, advanced integrations, or priority support in the future. However, the core platform will remain free forever. Early adopters will always have access to all existing features at no cost.
